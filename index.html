Creator & Developer

Transient Nuclear Reactor Xe-135 Simulator

A visual, real-time representation of Xenon-135 behavior in a reactor core during load-following transients.

Technical details

  • Numerical solvers in JavaScript integrate the coupled Iodine-135 and Xenon-135 concentration equations over simulated time.
  • A control interface lets the operator move reactor power and watch the delayed isotopic response that follows.
  • Core dynamics are visualized to illustrate xenon-pit dead time and the risk of spatial power oscillation.
