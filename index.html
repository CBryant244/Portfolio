Creator & Developer

Transient Nuclear Reactor Xe-135 Simulator

This simulator provides a visual, real-time representation of Xenon-135 behavior within a nuclear reactor core during load-following transients.

Technical Details:

  • Implemented numerical solvers in JavaScript to integrate Iodine-135 and Xenon-135 concentration equations over simulated time.
  • Designed an intuitive control interface allowing users to adjust reactor power levels and observe the resulting delayed isotopic responses.
  • Visualized core dynamics to effectively illustrate the 'Xenon pit' dead-time and the risk of spatial power oscillations.
